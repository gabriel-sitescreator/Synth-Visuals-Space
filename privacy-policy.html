Privacy Policy

At Synth Visuals Space, accessible from https://synthvisuals.space, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Synth Visuals Space and how we use it.

1. Log Files

Synth Visuals Space follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting services do this as part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Synth Visuals Space uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

Our website contains affiliate links to third-party services and vendors (such as software creators and affiliate networks). When you click on these links, third-party cookies may be placed on your browser to track referrals for commission purposes. We do not have control over cookies used by third-party advertisers or partner sites.

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
