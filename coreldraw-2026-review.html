Corel also includes AI upsampling and JPEG artifact cleanup for preparing low-quality images before tracing or printing[cite: 3].

These new tools do a lot of the prep work for you, so you spend less time rebuilding client files and more time designing[cite: 3]. Under “What’s New,” Corel calls this mix of tools Artist Intelligence – using AI to speed routine tasks while keeping the artist in charge[cite: 3].

2. “Artist Intelligence”: Generative AI Meets Manual Precision

This release’s buzz phrase is Artist Intelligence – the idea that AI features should boost creativity without taking control[cite: 3]. In practice, CorelDRAW 2026 introduces a suite of generative-AI tools that integrate smoothly with traditional vector workflows[cite: 3].

AI Generate & Remix

A new “AI Generate” pane lets you type prompts to create original images[cite: 3]. Results are commercially licensed and fully editable in CorelDRAW[cite: 3]. You can fine-tune style (photorealistic, line art, 3D, pop art, etc.) and color palette, or use “Inspire me” to generate prompt suggestions[cite: 3]. After generating an image, the Remix option can transform it (for example, changing an autumn scene to summer) within seconds[cite: 3].

"TechRadar found these generative results 'astonishing' – the software could convert an autumn sunset to a sunny summer scene with no loss in detail."[cite: 3]

If an AI result isn’t perfect, you can still tweak it in CorelDRAW (vectorize with PowerTRACE, adjust shapes, etc.)[cite: 3].

AI-assisted Image Cleanup

CorelDRAW’s background removal is now one-click AI[cite: 3]. The tool instantly identifies the foreground (even tricky hair and fabric edges) and removes the rest[cite: 3]. In tests, the results were “impressive” – comparable to top photo editors[cite: 3]. Similarly, AI masking in PHOTO-PAINT auto-selects subjects and builds pixel-accurate masks that you can refine[cite: 3]. These let you isolate and replace parts of bitmaps far faster than manual selection[cite: 3]. Meanwhile, PowerTRACE (Corel’s bitmap-to-vector engine) uses AI upsampling to improve line art and logo tracing, converting low-res artwork into clean, editable vectors without a full redraw[cite: 3].

Workflow Impact

Together, these tools address common file headaches[cite: 3]. Corel says 2026 “addresses” low-res, artifacts, unwanted backgrounds, and non-editable elements in one go[cite: 3]. In a print shop scenario, that means your colleague’s fuzzy logo PNG or a client’s hair-bleed photo can be fixed in seconds – not hours[cite: 3]. For designers, it means more time sketching ideas and less time manual editing[cite: 3].

However, Corel stresses (and we concur) that these are assistive tools, not full replacements[cite: 3]. For example, while AI can draft an image from scratch, a human still must ensure brand colors, typography, and output settings are correct[cite: 3]. In short, generative AI is “fully integrated” and fun to use, but Corel’s goal is to inspire the artist, not replace them[cite: 3].

Credits and Limits

All AI generation uses Corel’s AI credit system[cite: 3]. Subscribers and maintenance customers get 2,000 credits per month (renewing) – enough for dozens of medium-sized images[cite: 3]. If you run out, credits are available for purchase (Corel’s pricing table shows packs from 2,000 to 35,000 credits)[cite: 3]. Note that credits don’t roll over between months, so heavy AI users must monitor usage[cite: 3]. Overall, the AI tools in 2026 are powerful and well-implemented, but they come with a metered system and require a modern GPU for best performance[cite: 3].
